## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project provides a comprehensive analysis of Shopify store performance, focusing on sales, customer behavior, product trends, revenue, returns, discounts, and overall operational performance.

The dashboard is designed to help business stakeholders monitor key performance indicators, identify business trends, understand customer purchasing patterns, and support data-driven decision-making.

The project demonstrates the complete Business Intelligence workflow, from data preparation and transformation to data modeling, DAX calculations, visualization, and business analysis.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

- Monitor sales performance and revenue trends
- Understand customer demographics and purchasing behavior
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Identify underperforming products

- Evaluate customer value and retention
- Analyze regional sales performance
- Compare performance across different time periods
- Monitor important business KPIs
- Identify potential revenue opportunities
- Support sales and marketing decisions
- Support data-driven operational planning

## 📌 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard focuses on important e-commerce KPIs including:

| KPI | Business Purpose |
|---|---|
| Gross Sales | Measures total sales generated before adjustments |
| Net Revenue | Measures revenue after applicable adjustments |
| Total Orders | Measures overall order volume |
| Average Order Value | Measures average revenue generated per order |
| Customer Lifetime Value | Measures long-term customer value |
| Customer Retention | Helps evaluate repeat customer behavior |
| Product Revenue | Measures revenue contribution by product |
| Quantity Sold | Measures product sales volume |
| Returns | Tracks returned orders/products |
| Discounts | Measures discount impact on sales |
| Regional Sales | Compares performance across regions |

📈 Analytical Approach

The analysis follows a structured approach:

1. Understand the Business Problem

Identify the key business areas requiring analysis, including sales, customers, products, regions, orders, returns, and discounts.

2. Prepare the Data

Clean and transform the available source data using Power Query and other data preparation techniques.

3. Build the Data Model

4. Develop DAX Measures

Create the required calculations and KPIs to measure business performance.

Build interactive visualizations that allow users to explore the data efficiently.
